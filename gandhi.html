Community Immersion — GP Karian
A reflective documentation of the immersion in Chamba — observations, conversations, and learnings from living with the host family.

Over time, I became a part of their daily life — helping with chores, cleaning the cowshed, delivering newspapers, and working in the fields alongside them.

What began as a part of the Gandhi Fellowship soon became much more than an assignment. It was an experience that touched me deeply and continues to stay with me.

The relationship I built with the family is one I will always cherish, and fortunately, we are still connected today.

02 — School Infrastructure

Transformation through
resilience.

GCPS Rajera, HP

The school infrastructure had been damaged for years, and despite continuous efforts by the teachers, the funds had still not been approved. During my Gandhi Fellowship, this school came under my responsibility. After seeing the poor condition of the classrooms and washrooms, I took the initiative to address the issue.

I conducted multiple meetings with the Pradhan to help get the funds approved and, even after the approval, continued following up regularly through calls and meetings to ensure the process moved forward.

By the end of January, the repair work had finally begun. Today, the school has improved classrooms and better washroom facilities, creating a more dignified learning environment for the students.

03 — Youth & Wellbeing

A session on social & emotional well-being.

300+ students

Conducted a session on Social and Emotional Well-being for over 300 students at Chamba University.

Mental health and emotional well-being have always been areas of deep interest for me. Through this session, I aimed to encourage meaningful conversations around self-awareness, emotional resilience, and the importance of seeking support when needed. It was an opportunity to engage with young people on a subject that I believe is essential for both personal and academic growth.
